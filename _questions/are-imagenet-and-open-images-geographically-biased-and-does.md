description: "Yes. In No Classification without Representation (NIPS 2017 workshop on ML for the developing world), we geolocated images in both datasets. About 45% of the ImageNet sample and over 32% of the Open Images sample came from the US, while China and India together held about 3%. Models trained on them scored bridegroom photos from Ethiopia and Pakistan less consistently than ones from the US and Australia."
