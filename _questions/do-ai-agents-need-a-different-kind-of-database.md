description: "Likely yes, because agents query far more often and far more speculatively than people. In Supporting Our AI Overlords (CIDR 2026), we call this agentic speculation: many exploratory, often redundant requests on the way to an answer. We argue for databases that accept \"probes\" with natural-language goals, share work across them, answer approximately when that is enough, and steer agents with hints."
