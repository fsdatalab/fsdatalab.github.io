## Work with us

We work with people who need to analyze large amounts of unstructured data but have no engineering team to build a solution. Unstructured data means text that doesn't fit in a spreadsheet, e.g., documents, interview transcripts, case files, or reports.

For example, public defenders and climate scientists use [DocETL](https://docetl.org), a UC Berkeley [EPIC Data Lab](https://epic.berkeley.edu) project that we work on with our friends at EPIC, to pull facts out of thousands of documents with LLMs. The people we work with tell us what their analysis needs, and we use what we learn to design our research systems.

### How to reach us

Email [shreyashankar@cmu.edu](mailto:shreyashankar@cmu.edu). In the email, describe your data (what kind of documents and roughly how many), and the question you want to answer with the data.

We are a research lab at Carnegie Mellon University, led by [Shreya Shankar](https://sh-reya.com), and the systems we build are open source.
