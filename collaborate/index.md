## Work with us

We'd love to work with you if you have unstructured data analysis needs, e.g., documents, transcripts, or reports, but no engineering team to build a solution. Email [shreyashankar@cmu.edu](mailto:shreyashankar@cmu.edu) with what kind of documents you have, roughly how many, and the question you want the documents to answer.

Public defenders and climate scientists already use [DocETL](https://docetl.org) to pull facts out of thousands of documents with LLMs. DocETL is a UC Berkeley [EPIC Data Lab](https://epic.berkeley.edu) project, which we work on with our friends at EPIC.

The people we work with tell us what their analysis needs, and we use what we learn to design our research systems. We are a CS research lab at Carnegie Mellon University, led by [Shreya Shankar](https://sh-reya.com), and the systems we build are open source.
