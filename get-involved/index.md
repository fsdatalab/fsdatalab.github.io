<div class="post-header"><h1>Get involved</h1></div>

Want to do research with us? Use [Quail](https://github.com/fsdatalab/quail)
to analyze a dataset, then email [shreyashankar@cmu.edu](mailto:shreyashankar@cmu.edu)
a short video of you explaining your analysis (feel free to make a
dashboard), a link to your code, and your CV.

You can run Quail on [Modal](https://modal.com/pricing), whose free plan
includes $30 of compute per month. See the [Modal
quickstart](https://github.com/fsdatalab/quail/blob/d8d31f14f9d5c40d6cf683a74ba860788ce0d500/demos/quickstart_modal.py).

## What makes a project good

Any one of these, anywhere in the stack:

- An interesting application that helps people, especially outside CS.
- A type of query that Quail cannot answer.
- A way to make Quail queries more efficient.
- A better way to write Quail queries, or to communicate their results to
  people, e.g., an interface, a dashboard, or a story.

## Ideas

- Which arguments in public comments on a federal rule does the final rule
  never address? ([Regulations.gov](https://www.regulations.gov/))
- Which vehicle defects showed up in owner complaints long before a recall?
  ([NHTSA complaints](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/nhtsas-office-of-defects-investigation-odi-complaints-complaints-flat-file))
- Which practices by banks, lenders, and debt collectors do consumers
  complain about across many companies?
  ([CFPB complaints](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/data-research/consumer-complaints/))
- Which state bills copy each other, and where is model legislation
  spreading? ([Open States](https://open.pluralpolicy.com/data/))
- How does police discipline for the same allegations differ across
  California agencies? ([Police Records Access Project](https://bids.berkeley.edu/california-police-records-access-project))
- How are courts responding to lawyers who cite cases that AI made up?
  ([CourtListener](https://www.courtlistener.com/help/api/bulk-data/))
- What causes aviation safety incidents, and how has that changed since 1988?
  ([NASA ASRS](https://asrs.arc.nasa.gov/search/database.html))
- What leads to amputations and hospitalizations at work?
  ([OSHA severe injury reports](https://www.osha.gov/severe-injury-reports))
- Which clinical trials could a given patient join?
  ([ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/))
- How have companies' descriptions of climate and cyber risk changed?
  ([10-K filings](https://huggingface.co/datasets/eloukas/edgar-corpus))
- How did coverage of an event, like the 1918 flu, spread across American
  newspapers? ([American Stories](https://huggingface.co/datasets/dell-research-harvard/AmericanStories))
- What do people actually use chatbots for?
  ([WildChat](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/WildChat-4.8M))
- Why do coding agents fail?
  ([SWE-agent trajectories](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nebius/SWE-agent-trajectories))

Or bring your own data and question.
